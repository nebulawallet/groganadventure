ALDERSPRINGS LANE · LAKE NORMAN, NC

Eli Grogan loves three things: Agar.io, Roblox, and dressing up as a dog.

But this morning, Eli woke up to find his beloved dog costume... GONE. In its place: a single glittery business card that read "SHIVE: HYPERPOP LEGEND. FUTURE DOG."

Shive, the cocky hyperpop wizard, has been stealing every dog costume around Lake Norman. He dreams of becoming a real dog, and his goons, dressed in dog costumes of their own, now roam the lake.

Eli is angry. With help from his friends, he's going to hunt Shive down and take back what's his.
